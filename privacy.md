Privacy Policy
Personal Health Trends is a personal-use application.

The application accesses Oura data only after account-owner authorization. Authorized data may include sleep, heart rate, and heart rate variability information.

The application is designed to process and store retrieved data locally on the account owner's computer. It does not operate a public data collection service, sell personal data, or intentionally transmit health data to third parties.

The account owner may revoke access through Oura's authorized application controls. Locally exported data can be deleted by the account owner.

This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change.
